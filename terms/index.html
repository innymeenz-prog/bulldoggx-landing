5. Wallets, USDC and the escrow contract

Your wallet is yours. Funds you use on BulldogGX are held as USDC in a wallet that you control. BulldogGX does not hold customer balances, does not issue account credits and cannot move funds out of your wallet without a transaction you authorize.

Entry fees are held by a smart contract. When you enter a paid competition, you authorize a transfer of the disclosed entry fee from your wallet into a BulldogGX escrow smart contract on the Base network. The contract holds both players' entry fees until the competition is settled under the Competition Rules, then pays the prize (both entry fees, less the disclosed platform fee) to the winner's wallet. The address of the deployed contract is published in the Service.

What you are authorizing. By confirming an entry you instruct BulldogGX to administer that competition and to submit the result, settlement, refund or void that the Competition Rules permit for it, including settlement that executes without a further signature from you. This authorization is limited to that competition and its disclosed fee. It does not authorize any other transfer.

Administrative powers, stated plainly. The escrow contract has administrative and arbiter roles. On the production contract, administrative control is held by a multi-signature wallet requiring two of three independent signers. The administrator can pause new competitions, void an unresolved competition (which refunds both entry fees), recover tokens sent to the contract by mistake, and upgrade the contract's code. The arbiter can decide a disputed competition in favor of one of its two players and nothing else. Neither role can take an entry fee held in escrow for itself, pay an entry fee to anyone other than one of the two players, or change the fee on a committed entry.

Blockchain realities. A completed on-chain transaction cannot be reversed by BulldogGX. Network congestion, outages, gas sponsorship failures or contract defects can delay settlement. When a match is resolved, the contract pays the winner automatically in the same transaction. USDC is a digital asset; while it is designed to track the U.S. dollar, its value is not guaranteed by BulldogGX and a wallet balance is not a bank deposit. Sending an unsupported token or using the wrong network can cause loss we cannot recover.

6. Adding funds and cashing out

BulldogGX does not sell USDC, does not accept card or bank payments, and does not pay anyone out in dollars. Money moves in and out of your wallet only between you and a licensed third-party provider, or between your wallet and another wallet you control.

Adding funds. You can add USDC to your wallet by transferring it from an external wallet or exchange, or by tapping Add Funds and buying USDC from a licensed third-party provider (an "onramp") with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another method it offers. The onramp sells the USDC to you directly under its own terms, performs its own checks, delivers the USDC to the wallet you control, and handles its own refunds and payment disputes. BulldogGX is not a party to that purchase and does not receive your payment.

Cashing out. USDC in your wallet is yours, and you can send it to any wallet or exchange that supports USDC on the Base network at any time. Where the Service offers Cash Out, you can sell USDC to a licensed third-party provider (an "off-ramp") that pays you in dollars to your own debit card or bank account. Your USDC moves directly from your wallet to the off-ramp, which sets its own fees, limits, payout times and identity checks under its own terms. BulldogGX never receives your USDC or your dollars. The provider's fee and expected payout time are shown before you confirm.

Providers, payment methods and payout options are available only where enabled for your account and location. A displayed or announced integration is not a promise that it will be available to you.

Buying USDC is separate from entering a competition. Funding a wallet does not enter a match, and losing a match is not grounds to dispute the purchase with your card issuer. You keep every lawful right to report an unauthorized charge. See the Refund, Cancellation and Payout Policy for how entry fees, refunds and payouts work.

9. Disputes, challenge windows, refunds and cancellation

Result challenges. When one player declares a result, the other has a challenge window (currently 72 hours, shown in the match record) to confirm or dispute it. If the window closes with no dispute, the declared result settles. If both players dispute each other's declarations, the match goes to BulldogGX review, and a BulldogGX arbiter decides it based on the verification recordings and other evidence. A match under review that is not decided within the period stated in the Competition Rules (currently 14 days) may be voided, which refunds both entry fees.

Review is competition administration, not legal arbitration. A BulldogGX reviewer or on-chain arbiter decides who won a game under the Competition Rules. That is different from the legal arbitration in section 17, which resolves legal claims between you and BulldogGX.

Cancellation. You may cancel a competition only when its state and rules allow it (for example, before an opponent has paid their entry fee). Once both entry fees are in escrow, unilateral cancellation is not available. If BulldogGX cancels or voids a competition before it is decided, both entry fees are returned and no platform fee is charged.

Corrections. You may ask support to review a suspected administrative error and may submit new material evidence. We will correct our own errors where we can, but we cannot reverse a completed on-chain payout. An internal decision governs the competition; it does not waive your legal rights, and missing an in-app deadline does not shorten any statutory period for a legal claim.

No arbitrary forfeiture. Any withholding, offset or forfeiture must have a basis in this Agreement, the Competition Rules or law, and we will tell you the basis. Restricting an account does not itself forfeit assets in your wallet, which remain under your control.
